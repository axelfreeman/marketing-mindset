Give your AI agent a marketing mindset, not a template

Short answer: marketing-mindset: an open-source skill that gives agents decision rules for marketing - thresholds, kill rules, positioning, offers, outreach.

An agent with a template writes you another landing page. An agent with a mindset tells you the page is not the problem, the offer is.

What the skill is

marketing-mindset is an open-source (MIT) skill file for AI agents - Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, ChatGPT and anything else that reads a skill or rule file. It is not a set of templates. It is the operating layer: how to frame a problem, what to measure, when to stop, and what to say.

What is inside

  • Volume floors and pre-registered kill rules for tests (50-100 / 300+ / three weeks).
  • Positioning and offer construction before tactics.
  • Outreach and cold-email judgement, including the "50-send rule is wrong" argument.
  • How to evaluate a marketing idea, a plan, or a launch without defaulting to cached LLM doctrine.

Why it beats prompting harder

Reusable decision rules beat one-off prompts because they survive the next task. Your agent stops producing confident summaries of a 20-send test and starts refusing to judge until the sample exists.
