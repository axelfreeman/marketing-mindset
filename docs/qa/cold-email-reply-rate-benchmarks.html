What is a good cold email reply rate?

Short answer: 1-3% replies on cold outbound is normal, 5-10% means list and offer are aligned, and anything above 10% usually was not cold. Compare against your own baseline, not a blog number.

The number everyone quotes comes from someone else's list, someone else's offer and someone else's definition of "reply".

The ranges that survive contact with reality

Reply rateWhat it usually means
0-1%Deliverability, list quality or offer mismatch. Fix before writing another variant.
1-3%Normal cold outbound. Workable if your deal size covers the cost per send.
3-5%List and offer are aligned. This is the band where iteration pays.
5-10%Either warm-ish traffic or a very tight ICP. Protect whatever produced it.
10%+Usually not cold: referrals, existing relationships, or a tiny hand-picked list.

Three things that change the number

  • What you count as a reply. "Unsubscribe" and "stop emailing me" are not the same as "tell me more". Count both, report separately.
  • Volume. At 40 sends a single extra reply is 2.5 percentage points. Below ~100 sends the rate is a rumour.
  • List source. A scraped list and a hand-built list of 200 names produce numbers that are not comparable.

What to do with your number

Write it down before you read it: sends, replies, positive replies, and the threshold at which you change something. Then change one thing at a time - subject line, opener, offer, list - and judge each after enough volume to see the effect. If your send limit is below that volume, you are not running a test, you are guessing with a dashboard open.
