Marketing Q&A

Short, direct answers with the numbers behind them.

  • How many emails before you can trust a channel? - 50-100 sends per variant before you judge a channel; 300+ before you judge a message; three weeks before you judge a cadence.
  • Marketing test sample size: how many before you decide - Decide the threshold before you look at the result: 50-100 units per channel, 300+ per message, three weeks per cadence.
  • Give your AI agent a marketing mindset, not a template - marketing-mindset: an open-source skill that gives agents decision rules for marketing - thresholds, kill rules, positioning, offers, outreach.
