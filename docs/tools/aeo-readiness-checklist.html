How to read the score

Under 30%: the site is invisible to answer engines for structural reasons, not for lack of content. 30-60%: crawlable, but not quotable - usually missing direct answers, numbers and entity signals. 60-80%: quoted occasionally; the gap is almost always third-party presence. Above 80%: you have done the cheap part; the rest is publishing cadence and being right more often than the competition.

Two checks carry more weight than the rest for a reason: one question answered in the first 60 words, and prices and limits stated as numbers. Models quote what they can verify, and vague pages get summarised into nothing.
