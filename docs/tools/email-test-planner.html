How many sends does a test need before the number means anything?

Most "our cold email got 9% replies" claims are noise: three replies out of 40 sends. Put your real baseline and the lift you care about below and get the volume a two-variant test needs. Nothing leaves the browser.

Fill the numbers and hit calculate.

How to use this without fooling yourself

  • Decide the kill rule before you look: "if after N sends B is not ahead, the channel is dead".
  • If your sending capacity is below the number above, the honest move is to test a bigger gap (e.g. 2% → 6%), not to read a smaller sample.
  • One variable at a time. Subject line and offer and list in the same test tells you nothing.
