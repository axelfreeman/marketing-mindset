Marketing engineer job description generator

Most JDs for this role are a marketer's JD with "engineer" typed over the title — and then the hire fails in month three because nobody wrote down what should exist at the end of it. Pick the level and the surface, and this generates the version that names artifacts, numbers and kill rules instead of "passion for growth".
