The four stop rules

  • Hard bounce rate above 2% in a day: stop the day and find the source of the addresses.
  • Spam complaints above 0.1%: stop the campaign, not just the day.
  • Any reply ends that person's sequence — the reply is the goal, not an interruption.
  • Opens halving while sends stay flat: drop back two ramp days before continuing.

How the ramp works

Day 1 starts at 20 sends (or your target, if it is smaller) and each following day is 50% more than the previous one, rounded, and never above the target. That is the shape most receiving filters expect from a domain that is building history. If you have several mailboxes, split the day's volume between them — one mailbox pushing the whole day is a signature, not a strategy.

Check the numbers before you send

The schedule tells you when you reach full volume; the test-size planner tells you how many contacts an honest test needs; the kill-rule calculator tells you when to stop. Use all three before the first send, not after the third.
